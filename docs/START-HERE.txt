A9 Camera for Mac — version 0.3.3 (experimental public preview)

Requires Apple Silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26 or later.
This app is ad hoc signed and is not Apple notarized.
It enables macOS App Sandbox and Hardened Runtime.

Extract the ZIP and open A9 Camera.app. macOS may block a downloaded copy;
approve this specific app in Privacy & Security only if you trust it.
Bluetooth and Local Network access are used for their respective features.
Location Services is not required.

DIRECT CAMERA WI-FI
1. Power on your camera.
2. Use macOS Wi-Fi settings to join its hotspot (often Nax_..., A9, or V720).
3. In Camera Wi-Fi, click I joined camera Wi-Fi — connect.
   Default: 192.168.169.1, Automatic (classic first), TCP port 6123.
4. Try the other V720 protocol or a different local IP if your camera needs it.
5. Save snapshot saves the current JPEG; Stop ends the stream.

The Wi-Fi buttons open settings. They do not join a network automatically.
Joining the camera hotspot may interrupt internet access on the Mac.

HOME WI-FI SETUP
Choose Bluetooth, scan for a live camera and connect; or choose Camera Wi-Fi
(classic V720), join the camera hotspot manually, and enter its IP on the right.
Enter the 2.4 GHz SSID/password and check the recipient before sending.
Acceptance of settings does not verify a successful network join. A disconnect
without acknowledgement is unconfirmed. Check the router before resending.
Rejoin home Wi-Fi afterward; some firmware requires a separate local gateway
for video even if the camera has a router IP. The app does not provide it.

TEST STATUS
Packet parsing, both MJPEG handshakes, failure handling, timeout, cancellation,
and the GUI are tested using simulated cameras. See TEST-RESULTS.md.
Physical-camera streaming was not revalidated for this release.
The supplied 0.3.0 guide reports a historical camera test on 2026-10-11;
that report is not new test evidence for this release.

LIMITS
Home Wi-Fi provisioning and streaming through a router remain experimental.
Some camera firmware needs vendor registration or extra local server setup.
H.264, audio, recording, AI detection, motion alerts, and SD-card management
are not implemented. No firmware images or firmware updater are included.
No personal camera identity or Wi-Fi password is bundled with this release.

SECURITY
The camera TCP protocol is unencrypted and does not verify camera identity.
Bluetooth link encryption has not been verified. Use a dedicated camera
network and only send credentials to a device you recognize.
See SECURITY-REVIEW.md for the hardening changes and remaining risks.
